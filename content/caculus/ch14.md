设$D(t) = \left\{(x,y) \,\middle|\, 2x^2 + 3y^2 \le 6t\right\} (t \ge 0)$,

$$f(x,y) = \begin{cases} \dfrac{\sqrt[3]{1 - \left(x^2 + y^2\right)} - 1}{e^{\left(x^2 + y^2\right)} - 1} & (x,y) \neq (0,0)\\ a & (x,y) = (0,0) \end{cases}$$

为连续函数,令$F(t) = \displaystyle\iint_{D(t)} f(x,y)\, \mathrm{d}x\, \mathrm{d}y$,则$F'_+(0) = $＿＿＿

<!-- answer -->
首先计算出$a$的值.令$x = r \cos \theta, y = r \sin \theta,$则$\lim\limits_{\substack{x\to 0\\ y\to 0}} \Longleftrightarrow \lim\limits_{r\to 0}$

$$\lim_{\substack{x\to 0\\ y\to 0}} f(x,y) = \lim_{r\to 0} \frac{\sqrt[3]{1-r^2} - 1}{e^{r^2} - 1} = \frac{1 - \frac{1}{3} r^2 - 1}{r^2 + 1 - 1} = -\frac{1}{3}$$

故$a = -\dfrac{1}{3}$

$$F'_+(0) = \lim_{t\to 0^+} \frac{F(t) - F(0)}{t} = \lim_{t\to 0^+} \frac{F(t)}{t} = \lim_{t\to 0^+} \frac{A(t)\, f(\xi, \eta)}{t} = \lim_{t\to 0^+} \sqrt{6}\, \pi\, f(\xi, \eta) = - \frac{\sqrt{6}\, \pi}{3}$$

**【重点】（椭圆面积的计算）**

- 标准形式,表达式为$\dfrac{x^2}{a^2} + \dfrac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$

  $$S = 4 \int_0^a \frac{b}{a} \sqrt{a^2 - x^2}\, \mathrm{d}x = \pi a b$$

- 标准参数形式,表达式为$\begin{cases} x = a \cos \theta\\ y = b \sin \theta \end{cases} \quad \theta \in [0, 2\pi)$

  $$S = \frac{1}{2} \oint_C \left(x\, \mathrm{d}y - y\, \mathrm{d}x\right) = \frac{1}{2} \int_0^{2\pi} \left(a \cos \theta \cdot b \cos \theta - b \sin \theta \cdot \left(-a \sin \theta\right)\right) \mathrm{d}\theta = \pi a b$$

- 广义极坐标形式,表达式为$\begin{cases} x = a r \cos \theta\\ y = b r \sin \theta \end{cases} \quad r \in [0,1], \theta \in [0, 2\pi)$

  $$S = \iint_D \mathrm{d}x\, \mathrm{d}y = \int_0^{2\pi} \int_0^1 \left|J\right| \mathrm{d}r\, \mathrm{d}\theta = \pi a b$$

- 一般形式,表达式为$A x^2 + B x y + C y^2 + D x + E y + F = 0$,其中要求$A, C$不全为零且判别式$\Delta = B^2 - 4 A C < 0$.在该形式下,通常通过配方法或正交变换化为标准形式再求面积

<!-- stem -->
设平面有界区域$D$位于第一象限,由曲线$x^2 + y^2 - x y = 1, x^2 + y^2 - x y = 2$与直线$y = \sqrt{3} x, \ y = 0$围成,计算$\displaystyle\iint_D \frac{1}{3 x^2 + y^2}\, \mathrm{d}x\, \mathrm{d}y$

<!-- answer -->
对于曲线$x^2 + y^2 - x y = 1$和$x^2 + y^2 - x y = 2$,将$x, y$互换后式子不变,故其关于$y = x$对称,根据判别式确定其为椭圆图像,画出草图后,将直角坐标系转化为极坐标系,利用图像确定$r, \theta$的取值范围:

$$\begin{aligned}
&x^2 + y^2 - x y = 1 \Longrightarrow r^2 - r^2 \sin \theta \cos \theta = 1, \quad r = \sqrt{\frac{1}{1 - \sin \theta \cos \theta}}\\
&x^2 + y^2 - x y = 2 \Longrightarrow r^2 - r^2 \sin \theta \cos \theta = 2, \quad r = \sqrt{\frac{2}{1 - \sin \theta \cos \theta}}\\
&y = \sqrt{3} x \Longrightarrow \theta = \frac{\pi}{3}\\
&y = 0 \Longrightarrow \theta = 0
\end{aligned}$$

故:

$$\begin{aligned}
\iint_D \frac{1}{3 x^2 + y^2}\, \mathrm{d}x\, \mathrm{d}y &= \int_0^{\pi/3} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_{\sqrt{\frac{1}{1 - \sin \theta \cos \theta}}}^{\sqrt{\frac{2}{1 - \sin \theta \cos \theta}}} \frac{1}{r^2 \left(3 \cos^2 \theta + \sin^2 \theta\right)}\, r\, \mathrm{d}r\\
&= \int_0^{\pi/3} \frac{1}{3 \cos^2 \theta + \sin^2 \theta}\, \mathrm{d}\theta \cdot \ln r \bigg|_{\sqrt{\frac{1}{1 - \sin \theta \cos \theta}}}^{\sqrt{\frac{2}{1 - \sin \theta \cos \theta}}}\\
&= \frac{\ln 2}{2} \int_0^{\pi/3} \frac{1}{3 \cos^2 \theta + \sin^2 \theta}\, \mathrm{d}\theta = \frac{\ln 2}{2} \int_0^{\pi/3} \frac{1}{3 + \tan^2 \theta}\, \mathrm{d}(\tan \theta)\\
&= \frac{\ln 2}{2} \left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \arctan\left(\frac{\tan \theta}{\sqrt{3}}\right)\right) \Big|_0^{\frac{\pi}{3}}\\
&= \frac{\sqrt{3} \ln 2}{24} \pi
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- q {"id": "calc-ch14-q4", "type": "练习", "tags": ["二重积分"], "title": "极坐标转直角坐标交换次序"} -->

<!-- stem -->
计算二重积分$I = \displaystyle\iint_D r^2 e^{- r^2 \sin^2 \theta} \cos \theta\, \mathrm{d}r\, \mathrm{d}\theta,$其中$D = \left\{(r, \theta) \,\middle|\, r \ge 0, \ \dfrac{\pi}{4} \le \theta \le \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right\}$

<!-- answer -->
将极坐标系转化为直角坐标系,则:

$$\begin{aligned}
\iint_D r^2 e^{- r^2 \sin^2 \theta} \cos \theta\, \mathrm{d}r\, \mathrm{d}\theta &= \int_0^{+\infty} \mathrm{d}x \int_x^{+\infty} x \cdot e^{-y^2}\, \mathrm{d}y \quad \text{（不好积分,尝试交换积分次序）}\\
&= \int_0^{+\infty} \mathrm{d}y \int_0^{y} x \cdot e^{-y^2}\, \mathrm{d}x\\
&= \frac{1}{2} \int_0^{+\infty} y^2 e^{-y^2}\, \mathrm{d}y \quad \text{（令 } t = y^2\text{）}\\
&= \frac{1}{2} \int_0^{+\infty} t \cdot e^{-t} \cdot \frac{1}{2 \sqrt{t}}\, \mathrm{d}t\\
&= \frac{1}{4} \operatorname{Tau}\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)\\
&= \frac{1}{8} \operatorname{Tau}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = \frac{1}{8} \sqrt{\pi}
\end{aligned}$$
