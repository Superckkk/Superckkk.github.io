<!-- answer -->
易知：只有当$n = 1$时，$f(x) = \tan \dfrac{\pi x^1}{4} - 1 = 0$.因此，

$$\begin{aligned}
f(x) &= \left(\tan \frac{\pi x}{4} - 1\right) \cdot \prod_{n = 2}^{100} \left(\tan \frac{\pi x^n}{4} - n\right) = \tan\left(\frac{\pi x}{4} - 1\right) \cdot g(x)\\
f'(x) &= \frac{\pi}{4} \sec^2 \frac{\pi x}{4} \cdot g(x) + \tan\left(\frac{\pi x}{4} - 1\right) \cdot g'(x)\\
f'(1) &= \frac{\pi}{4} \sec^2 \frac{\pi}{4} \cdot g(1) = - \frac{\pi \cdot 99!}{2}
\end{aligned}$$
