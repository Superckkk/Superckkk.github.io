<!-- q {"id": "calc-ch10-q1", "type": "练习", "tags": ["定积分的几何应用"], "title": "摆线一拱与 x 轴围成的面积"} -->

<!-- stem -->
计算由摆线$\begin{cases} x = r(t - \sin t)\\ y = r(1 - \cos t) \end{cases} (r>0)$的一拱与$x$轴所围平面图形的面积.

<!-- answer -->
> 〔原文此处为 Typst 矢量示意图：摆线的函数图像〕

对于参数方程，我们同样可以代入到平面直角坐标系的公式中计算:

$$\begin{aligned}
S = \int_0^{2\pi r} y(t)\, \mathrm{d}(x(t)) &= \int_0^{2\pi} r(1-\cos t) \cdot x'(t)\, \mathrm{d}t\\
&= \int_0^{2\pi} r^2(1-\cos t)^2 \mathrm{d}t\\
&= r^2 \int_0^{2\pi} 1\, \mathrm{d}t - 2r^2 \int_0^{2\pi} \cos t\, \mathrm{d}t + r^2 \int_0^{2\pi} \cos^2 t\, \mathrm{d}t\\
&= 2\pi r^2 + r^2 \cdot 4 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{\pi}{2} \quad \text{（点火公式）}\\
&= 3 \pi r^2
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- q {"id": "calc-ch10-q2", "type": "练习", "tags": ["定积分的几何应用"], "title": "y = e^{-x}sin x 与 x 轴围成的面积"} -->

<!-- stem -->
求曲线$y = e^{-x} \sin x \ (x \ge 0)$与$x$轴所围平面图形的面积

<!-- answer -->
> 〔原文此处为 Typst 矢量示意图：$y = e^{-x} \sin x$ 的图像 $(x \ge 0)$，含包络线 $y = \pm e^{-x}$〕

易知：$y = e^{-x} \sin x$的图像是部分在$x$轴上方,部分在$x$轴下方的振荡图像，因此，要求函数图像与$x$轴所围的面积,实际是求$\int_0^{+\infty} \left|e^{-x} \sin x\right| \mathrm{d}x$:

$$\begin{aligned}
\int_0^{+\infty} \left|e^{-x} \sin x\right| \mathrm{d}x &= \lim_{n\to\infty} \sum_{i=0}^n \int_{i\pi}^{(i+1)\pi} \left|e^{-x} \sin x\right| \mathrm{d}x\\
&= \lim_{n\to\infty} \sum_{i=0}^n \left| \int_{i\pi}^{(i+1)\pi} e^{-x} \sin x\, \mathrm{d}x\right|\\
&= \lim_{n\to\infty} \sum_{i=0}^n \left| \frac{(-1)^i \left(e^{\pi} + 1\right)}{2 e^{(i+1) \pi}}\right|\\
&= \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{e^{\pi} + 1}{2} \sum_{i=0}^n \frac{1}{e^{(i+1) \pi}}\\
&= \frac{1+e^{\pi}}{2\left(e^{\pi} - 1\right)} \quad \text{（等比数列求和公式）}
\end{aligned}$$

**【说明】** 第三个等号处使用了结论：

$$\int e^{a x} \sin b x\, \mathrm{d}x = \frac{\begin{vmatrix}\left(e^{a x}\right)' & \left(\sin b x\right)'\\ e^{a x} & \sin b x\end{vmatrix}}{a^2 + b^2} + C = \frac{a e^{a x} \sin b x - b e^{a x} \cos b x}{a^2 + b^2} + C$$

<!-- q {"id": "calc-ch10-q5", "type": "练习", "tags": ["定积分的几何应用"], "title": "变限积分函数的平均值"} -->

<!-- stem -->
已知函数$f(x) = x \displaystyle\int_1^x \frac{e^{t^2}}{t}\, \mathrm{d}t ,$则$f(x)$在$(0,1)$上的平均值为＿＿＿.

<!-- answer -->
$$\begin{aligned}
\int_0^1 f(x)\, \mathrm{d}x = \int_0^1 x \int_1^x \frac{e^{t^2}}{t}\, \mathrm{d}t\, \mathrm{d}x &= \frac{1}{2} \int_0^1 \int_1^x \frac{e^{t^2}}{t}\, \mathrm{d}t\, \mathrm{d}\left(x^2\right)\\
&= \frac{1}{2} \left(x^2 \int_1^x \frac{e^{t^2}}{t}\, \mathrm{d}t \Big|^x_1 - \int_0^1 x^2\, \mathrm{d}\left(\int_1^x \frac{e^{t^2}}{t}\, \mathrm{d}t\right)\right)\\
&= -\frac{1}{2} \int_0^1 x^2 \frac{e^{x^2}}{x}\, \mathrm{d}x\\
&= -\frac{1}{4} \int_0^1 e^{x^2}\, \mathrm{d}\left(x^2\right)\\
&= -\frac{1}{4}\, e^{x^2} \Big|^1_0 = \frac{1-e}{4}
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- stem -->
曲线$y = x^2$从点$(1,1)$到点$(2,4)$的一段弧线绕$y$轴旋转一周所得旋转体的侧面积为＿＿＿.

<!-- answer -->
① $y = x^2 \Rightarrow x = \sqrt{y}$,因此:

$$S = 2\pi \int_1^4 x(y) \sqrt{1 + \left(x'(y)\right)^2}\, \mathrm{d}y = 2 \pi \int_1^4 \sqrt{y + \frac{1}{4}}\, \mathrm{d}y = 2\pi \cdot \frac{2}{3} \left(y+\frac{1}{4}\right)^{3/2} \Big|^4_1 = \frac{\pi}{6} \left(17 \sqrt{17} - 5\sqrt{5}\right)$$
